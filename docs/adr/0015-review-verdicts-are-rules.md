# Monthly review verdicts are decided by rules; a model only words them

Carried over from ramnn, where two surfaces had derived their own verdicts and drifted within a week. A month's verdicts (a budget over, a purchase that outweighed the savings, money gone to untracked accounts, the savings target's status, nothing to report) and its tone are decided by one pure module from the review's facts and the reader's calendar day. A model may write the review narrative, once, from those facts and verdicts; a narrative stating a figure absent from the facts is dropped and the worded verdicts stand in.

## Consequences

- The same month always yields the same verdicts, on every surface and in every language.
- The review is computed for one member's view of the household, so each member gets their own.
